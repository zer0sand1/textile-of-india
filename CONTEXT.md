# Textiles of India

A curated, git-based reference site about India's textile traditions, built for a small group of friends to read and contribute to. Inspired by WikiRug (Persian/Oriental carpets), centred on an interactive map of India.

## Language

**Craft**:
A named textile tradition — the page-level entity of the site. Examples: Ajrakh, Chikankari, Kanjeevaram, Bandhani.
_Avoid_: textile type, category, style

**Technique**:
The broad making-category a Craft belongs to. Working set: weave, embroidery, block-print, resist-dye, hand-paint (extendable as new crafts demand it).
_Avoid_: textile (everything on the site is a textile — it names nothing)

**Place**:
A named geographic location with a granularity: state (an Indian state/UT), region (a cultural area within a state, e.g. Kutch), or town (e.g. Bagru). Regions and towns belong to a parent state. States are the clickable units of the map.
_Avoid_: location, area

**Origin**:
The role a Place plays for a Craft — where that tradition comes from. A Craft has one or more Origins (Bandhani → Kutch *and* Rajasthan).
_Avoid_: provenance, homeland

**Credit**:
The attribution attached to every image: author, license, and source URL (Wikimedia Commons, an open-access museum, or a friend's own photo). Shown alongside the image.
_Avoid_: caption, citation

**Source**:
A reference cited by a Craft page — linked inline (Wikipedia-style `[n]`) where claims rely on it and listed in full at the end of the page. Every Source must be a real page retrieved by the research pipeline (Tavily); model memory is never a Source.
_Avoid_: citation, footnote

**Correction**:
A reader-flagged error on a Craft page, submitted through the page's "Suggest an edit" button as a pre-filled GitHub issue and reviewed by the maintainer. The correction loop that backstops auto-published, AI-researched content.
_Avoid_: report, feedback

**Canon**:
The curated launch set of ~40–50 well-known Crafts, drafted by the research pipeline and approved once by the maintainer. The site launches when every Canon craft has a page.
_Avoid_: launch list

**Backlog**:
The long tail of textile-related GI-registered crafts beyond the Canon, turned into pages by the research pipeline over time after launch.
_Avoid_: roadmap
